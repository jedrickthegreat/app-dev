## About the Series

**One Piece** is a Japanese manga written and illustrated by *Eiichiro Oda*. It started in *Weekly Shonen Jump* in 1997 and was later adapted into an anime by Toei Animation. It follows **Monkey D. Luffy**, a young pirate whose goal is to find the legendary treasure called the *One Piece* and become the **Pirate King**.

## Why I Like It

1. The adventure and world-building are amazing.
2. Every crew member has their own dream and backstory.
3. The story balances comedy, action, and emotional moments.
